*For JD. Plain language, one command at the end.*

## What happened

The fifth outside audit (GPT Pro, Aug 1) arrived with three things: a clean
bill for everything we've certified, one real (harmless) flaw in cert 0021's
prose, and a complete proposed proof that **X = 7 forces m ≤ 24** — squeezing
the minimum-excess frontier from five edge counts down to three.

We ran the full intake law on it: the text went verbatim to three hostile
refuter lanes, statements-only to three blind lanes, and the desk re-derived
every step by hand before anything entered the chain. Ten Opus lanes total,
plus four more mining the two OpenAI documents you supplied.

**The result is cert 0022, green ×2: X = 7 ⟹ m ≤ 24.** The staircase now
reads: X ≥ 7 on m = 22–24 · **X ≥ 8 on m = 25–28** · X ≥ 9 at 29 · X ≥ 10
from 30. Fifth outside review, fifth theorem.

## The intake ledger — errors caught in both directions, again

- The audit's flaw-report on 0021 was **right** ("every high cell lies in T"
  is false in general). Numbers unchanged — erratum filed in 0021.
- But the audit's own **repair of that flaw had a hole**: its fix-lemma

  hand; all three refuters found it independently; one refuter then showed
  the missed pattern is impossible at m = 25/26 for a reason the audit never

  starts. Third consecutive intake where the numbers survived but a stated
  reason needed repair. The certificate now carries the honest case law with
  built witnesses.
- Two blind lanes **proved both rungs independently** without ever seeing
  the audit's text — the strongest independent-derivation coverage any cert
  in this repo has had.

## Two new tools came out of the fleet

1. **The tiling test** (found by a blind lane): the 36 cells split 6-per-part,
   and each part's degrees must sum to exactly m — so any candidate census
   must *tile*. Stronger than our census check. The next wall survives it
   (measured), but every future census now faces it.
2. **Quotient completeness** (from the OpenAI extremal-graph proof — the one
   document GPT itself never saw): a uniform way to *generate* every overlap
   pattern of a shape rather than remember them case by case. The apex hole
   above is exactly the kind of case it would have generated automatically.
