## What happened

GPT Pro's sixth review arrived ("Ryser-5"). It audited turn 19's
certificate, found four small prose defects, and proposed the biggest
jump yet: a global version of the part-collision law plus an exact sweep,
claiming X ≥ 10 everywhere. The intake law did its work — three hostile

  only possible on m ∈ [22, 25]** — from 26 up, X ≥ 11. Turn 18's
  frontier was seven rungs wide at X = 8; it is now **four rungs at
  X = 10**, and the exact list of surviving configurations (74 of them)

  The new chain never touches parity, ledgers, or quotients — if a flaw
  ever surfaced in one stack, the other holds the floor alone.

## The review, in both directions

Every number GPT proposed checked out — six independent recomputations,
one of them fully blind, with an explicit verified witness for every
surviving cell. But three of its **proof steps** were wrong: it credited
the wrong lemma for the X = 7 kill, and twice it asserted configurations
were "edge-disjoint" when the exceptional triangles are perfectly
realizable — the same gap-by-narration that bit the third and fifth
audits. The fix is structural now: certificate 0025 *generates* every
support configuration from graphs and kills each one by law; the missed
triangles are pinned in it as permanent regression checks.

One humbling catch on our side: GPT's diagnosis of a real 0023 prose
defect was itself faulty, and I briefly wrote its faulty reasoning into
the repair. One of our own hostile lanes caught the over-retraction the
same day; the erratum is now twice-corrected, both versions dated, and
the intake law gained a clause: proposed repairs of our own certificates
go through the refuters too.

## Where this leaves the campaign

A minimal counterexample now needs 22–456 edges **and** at least ten
units of pair-excess — and if it has exactly ten, at most 25 edges. The
next campaign is those four rungs; the machinery for them ((EC), (RG2),
the posture engine) is banked and named in PLAN.

Nothing here claims a counterexample exists, and Ryser r = 6 remains
open — we verified the current literature says the same (Clow–Haxell–
